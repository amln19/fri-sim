Protocol & measurement definitions
  • 20 saved trials: five each at 0, 100, 300, and 600 ms, in a shuffled order. The condition is revealed only after each trial.
  • Five matched encounters per condition. The other car starts 180° opposite your approach, already circulating counter-clockwise at 36–44 km/h. Your maximum speed is 48 km/h; acceleration is 2.4 m/s². Some encounters require yielding before accelerating into the circle. Redo repeats the same encounter and latency.
  • The tablet uses timestamped position history, interpolated at simulation time minus the assigned delay. A 120 Hz simulation isolates delay; no packet loss, extrapolation, or conformal safety assistance is applied in this human pilot.
  • Closest distance is the minimum horizontal clearance between the oriented vehicle-body rectangles (4.4 × 1.85 m), sampled at 120 Hz; zero means contact. Bounding boxes detect collisions.
  • Yield wait counts speed ≤ 1 km/h while the front bumper is within 3 m before, or 1 m after, the yield line. Score = 100 for a pass, or −500 for a crash, minus 10 × yield wait seconds. A timeout earns no completion bonus.
  • Paused time is excluded. Tab changes, focus loss, or long frame interruptions pause the trial. Restart an interrupted trial for a strict continuous run.
  • Save commits a result; discard removes the unsaved attempt. All saved sessions export together using the requested eight CSV columns. Results stay in this browser’s local storage. Setup 03 trial IDs begin with p3-; earlier setup results are retained, so filter these IDs when comparing the updated setup.
